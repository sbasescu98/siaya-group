About Us

Siaya Group dates back to 2024 when co-founders Nate and Simon met as economic researchers and policy advisors in Nairobi, Kenya. They developed a deep understanding of the technical, institutional, and political challenges faced by mission-driven teams. This experience led them to found Siaya Group, to develop and promote decisive, evidence-based research with real-world impact.

Siaya Group bridges the gap between rigorous analysis and tangible action. We turn complex information into on-the-ground action, guiding projects from initial strategy through to successful implementation. Whether working with a multilateral development bank, a national ministry, or a growth-stage business, we bring the same commitment to clarity, rigor, and results.
